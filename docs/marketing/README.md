## Why the UI mockups are not in this folder

Five images were generated for the store: two pieces of key art, and three
mockups of a game interface. The key art is here. The mockups are not, and
should not be uploaded as screenshots. Three reasons, in order of how much
trouble each would cause:

**They are a different game.** They are branded *Flip Empire — Property Tycoon*
and *Flip Empire — Property Simulator*. They show photographic property
listings, a real street map with price pins, before-and-after kitchen
photography, a reputation score, achievements, a crew and supplier roster, a
message inbox and a deal-refresh countdown. None of that exists in Property
Flipper, and most of it is not on the roadmap. A screenshot is a claim about
what the software looks like; key art is allowed to be a poster.

**One of them teaches the 70% rule wrong.** The renovation mockup labels
`$199,500` — 70% of a `$285,000` ARV — as the *maximum allowable rehab budget*.
Under the rule that figure is the maximum for everything: purchase **and**
rehab, less nothing. Shipping that on the page for a game whose entire purpose
is teaching people to underwrite honestly would be the worst possible first
impression, and the kind of error the audience it is aimed at will spot.

**They have the usual generated-text damage.** `Mупl. 10:15 AM`, `DOWNTOIXN`,
`BIOUIT PARKE`, `iNLL DIПACRIC`, and a reputation bar reading `3,450 / $50 / 53`.

The marketing plan that came with them says the same thing in its own pro tip:
replace the mockups with real screenshots once real ones exist. They exist —
ten of them, regenerated from the running game on every change, at the exact
size the embed uses.

**What the mockups are genuinely good for** is design direction, and there is
real signal in them: photographic property thumbnails, an explicit
before/after slider on the renovation screen, and a compliance meter that shows
how far a deal sits from the 70% line rather than only naming the number.
Those are ideas about the product, and they belong in the roadmap rather than
on the store page.

## Sources

`source/key-art-cutaway.png` and `source/key-art-before-after.png` are committed
at full size because they are the masters; everything above is derived from them
and can be thrown away.

A note on the art itself: it is photoreal, and the game is an isometric line and
colour drawing. That gap is normal for cover art and is not, by itself, a
problem — but it is worth knowing it is there, because the complaint an itch
page earns for promising one look and delivering another is hard to undo. The
screenshots directly under the banner are what keep it honest, which is another
reason they have to be the real thing.
